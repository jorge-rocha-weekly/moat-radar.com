<p>AI investments continue to grow at an extraordinary pace. Alphabet invested US$51.4 billion in a single quarter, while Anthropic took on US$895.4 billion in computing commitments for the years ahead.</p>
<p>It is still too early to know whether computing capacity will exceed demand. What is already clear is that the market is beginning to demand answers about returns on these investments.</p>
<p>The concern appears to be not just the size of Capex, but its effect on companies' cash flow and the speed with which these investments translate into revenue and results.</p>
